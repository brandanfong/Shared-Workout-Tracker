# How Scoring Works

This page explains how points are earned in the Shared Workout Tracker, from your own workouts to the group's weekly goals.

## Getting Started

**You earn no points until you set your goals.** Scoring only starts from the day you set them, so any workouts logged before that earn nothing, but are still displayed on the graph.

Your goals are three numbers: **minutes** per day, **calories** per day, and **complete workouts** per week. A complete workout is a day you hit both your minutes and your calories. Changing your goals only affects days from then on.

A **week** runs Monday to Sunday. Weekly bonuses and group results are calculated once the week is over.

## Your Own Points

Points come from two things: your calories and your complete workouts.

### Active Calories

| Period | You do this | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Hit your calorie goal | +1 |
| Daily | Hit 150% of it | +2 |
| Daily | Hit 200% of it | +3 |
| Weekly | Hit your calorie goal on 6 of 7 days | +3 |
| Weekly | Hit your calorie goal on all 7 days | +5 |
| Monthly | Hit your calorie goal on 80% / 90% / 100% of days | +5 / +10 / +20 |

### Complete Workouts

A complete workout is a day you hit both your minutes and your calories. Minutes don't earn points on their own.

| Period | You do this | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Log a complete workout | +2 |
| Weekly | Reach your weekly complete workout target | +4 |

### When Points Arrive

Points are paid once your data for a day is final, not after a fixed delay. Each sync sends today's and yesterday's totals, so a day is final once you have synced on a later day: that sync re-sent the day in full, so it can't change any more. If you fell asleep before logging, yesterday still gets picked up.

- **Daily points** arrive once you have synced the next day or later.
- **Your own weekly bonuses** (6/7 calorie days, your workout target) arrive once **you** have synced on the Monday after the week or later. Nobody else's sync holds them up.
- **The monthly bonus** is personal too: it arrives once you have synced after the month ends.
- **The team bonus, the streak and the multiplier** are group results, so they arrive once **everyone** playing has synced on the Monday after the week (or later). One person's late sync only holds up these.
- **If someone never syncs,** their week or month is scored anyway 7 days after it ends, and any days with no data count as zero.

When new points of yours arrive, a popup shows them the next time you open the site or come back to it.

## Group Goals

### A Qualified Week

You have a qualified week when both requirements are met:

- Hit your active calorie goal on at least **5 of the 7 days**.
- Reach your **own** weekly complete workout goal.

Everyone has their own workout goal, so a qualified week looks different for each person.

### Group Success

If **everyone** in the group has a qualified week, the group succeeds. A successful week earns two things:

- **Team bonus:** +4 points for every member.
- **Streak:** the week adds to the group's streak, which boosts everyone's points (see below).

If any member falls short, no team bonus is awarded and the multiplier resets to x1.0.

## Streaks and Multipliers

Every week the group succeeds adds one to its streak. From your second successful week in a row, everyone's points for that week get a multiplier, team bonus included. The longer the streak, the bigger it gets.

| Successful weeks in a row | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| 0 to 1 | x1.0 |
| 2 to 3 | x1.1 |
| 4 to 7 | x1.25 |
| 8 or more | x1.5 |

When your streak reaches a new level, the multiplier is raised to that value right away. The monthly calorie bonus is never multiplied.

## Joining Mid-Week

A new week starts on a **Monday**. If you set your goals on a Monday, you take part in the group's weekly goals right away.

If you set them any later in the week, your progress won't count toward the group until the following Monday. Your goals are built for a full week, so with only a few days left you may be unable to reach them no matter how hard you try. It wouldn't be fair to the group to let that hold up the team bonus or break the streak. In the meantime you still earn points individually, and the group's current multiplier applies to them. If the group succeeds that week, you still get the team bonus.
